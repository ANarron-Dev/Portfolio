Package Terms & Policies

Project scope

Each package includes only the deliverables specified in its description. Additional designs, concepts, or services may be quoted separately.

Design revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included. A revision round consists of one consolidated set of requested changes to the presented designs. Additional revisions or substantial changes to an approved design direction may incur an additional fee, subject to client approval.

Payment schedule

A 50% deposit is required before design work begins. The remaining 50% is due following final design approval and before delivery of production-ready files.

Project timeline

Estimated turnaround begins after the deposit, completed design brief, and required client materials are received. Delayed feedback, additional requests, or changes in project scope may extend the completion date.

Client materials

Clients are responsible for supplying accurate business information and any existing logos, images, or other materials required for the project. Clients must have permission to use materials they provide.

Final deliverables

Final files will be delivered electronically in the formats specified for the purchased package. Printing, physical products, advertising placement, and third-party licensing fees are not included.

Additional services

Requests outside the agreed package scope will be discussed and priced separately before additional work begins.

Design ownership & source files

Upon receipt of full payment, Narron Design Studio transfers to the client its copyright ownership in the approved, original final designs created specifically for the client’s project, excluding any third-party materials subject to separate licensing terms. Clients receive the final approved designs in the file formats specified in their selected package, including scalable vector logo files where applicable. Narron Design Studio retains its editable working files, including native Adobe Illustrator (.AI), Photoshop (.PSD), and other project files. These working files are not included in the package price or standard deliverables. Unused concepts, preliminary drafts, rejected designs, and internal working materials remain the property of Narron Design Studio. Third-party fonts, stock assets, and other licensed materials remain subject to their respective license agreements. Narron Design Studio may display completed work in its portfolio and promotional materials unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Cancellation & refund policy

Clients may request cancellation at any time by written notice. Before initial concept presentation, 50% of the initial project deposit will be refunded and the remaining 50% retained for preparation, planning, and design work. After initial concepts have been presented, the initial deposit becomes nonrefundable. Additional payments will be reviewed based on work completed and the project agreement. Cancellation does not transfer ownership of unfinished work, preliminary concepts, or unpaid designs. Applicable refunds will be returned to the original payment method.
