Before We Get Started

Each package includes the listed deliverables and two consolidated revision rounds. Additional work is quoted and approved separately. Editable working files are not included unless agreed in writing.

A 50% deposit starts your project, with the balance due after final approval and before delivery. Full project terms, including ownership and cancellation policies, are provided before work begins.

Printing, paid fonts, ad placement, social media management, website development and work outside the selected package are not included unless separately agreed. Timelines depend on timely client feedback and materials. Custom quotes are available.
